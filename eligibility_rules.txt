ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA

1. Premium Savings Account
- Age Requirement: 18 years or older.
- Residency Status: Resident individuals only (Minors and Non-Residents excluded).
- Balance Requirement: Must fund with a minimum of ₹10,000 at account opening.

2. Fixed Deposit (FD)
- Age Requirement: 18 years or older.
- Minimum Deposit: ₹10,000 one-time deposit.

3. Recurring Deposit (RD)
- Age Requirement: 18 years or older.
- Minimum Deposit: ₹1,000 per month.

4. Unsecured Personal Loan
- Age Requirement: 21 to 58 years at the time of application.
- Income Profile: Salaried individuals only (self-employed profiles are currently rejected).
- Income Requirement: Minimum net take-home salary of ₹30,000 per month.
- Credit Profile: Minimum CIBIL score of 700.

5. Travel Rewards Credit Card
- Age Requirement: 21 to 60 years.
- Income Profile: Salaried or self-employed.
- Income Requirement: Minimum verified income of ₹40,000 per month.
- Credit Profile: Minimum CIBIL score of 720.
