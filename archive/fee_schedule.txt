FEE & PENALTY SCHEDULE

1. Premium Savings Account
- Minimum Average Balance (MAB) Non-Maintenance Fee: ₹500 per month (if balance drops below ₹10,000).
- ATM Fees: ₹20 per transaction beyond the 5 free monthly withdrawals.
- Transfer Fees: ₹0 for RTGS/NEFT.

2. Fixed Deposit (FD)
- Premature Withdrawal Penalty: 1.0% reduction on the applicable interest rate for the period the deposit was held.

3. Recurring Deposit (RD)
- Missed Installment Penalty: ₹50 per missed monthly deposit.

4. Unsecured Personal Loan
- Processing Fee: 2.0% of the total loan amount (deducted at disbursement).
- Pre-closure Charge: 3.0% of the outstanding principal balance.
- Late EMI Penalty: ₹500 flat fee plus 2.0% penal interest per month on the overdue amount.

5. Travel Rewards Credit Card
- Annual Fee: ₹1,500 (billed in the first month).
- Late Payment Fee: ₹750 per billing cycle if minimum due is not met.
- Over-limit Fee: ₹500 per occurrence.
- Foreign Transaction Markup: 3.5% of the transaction value.
- Card Replacement Fee: ₹200.
