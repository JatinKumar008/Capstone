PRODUCT OVERVIEW & CORE TERMS

1. Premium Savings Account
- Interest Rate: 4.0% p.a. (compounded daily, paid quarterly).
- Transaction Limits: 5 free ATM withdrawals per month. NEFT and RTGS transfers are free.
- Account Type: Single or Joint.

2. Fixed Deposit (FD)
- Interest Rate: 7.0% p.a. base rate. Senior citizens (60+) receive an additional 0.5% p.a.
- Tenure: 1 year to 5 years.
- Features: Auto-renewal option available at maturity. Premature withdrawal permitted subject to penalty.

3. Recurring Deposit (RD)
- Interest Rate: 6.5% p.a.
- Tenure: 12 months to 36 months.
- Deposit Limits: Maximum monthly deposit of ₹50,000. 
- Features: 3-month absolute lock-in period. Account closes automatically after 3 consecutive missed installments.

4. Unsecured Personal Loan
- Interest Rate: 10.5% to 15.0% p.a. (fixed, based on credit profile).
- Loan Amount: Minimum ₹50,000; Maximum ₹1,000,000.
- Tenure: 1 year to 5 years.
- Pre-closure: Allowed only after completing 12 months of EMI payments.

5. Travel Rewards Credit Card
- Core Rewards: 5x points on flight bookings, 1% cashback on all other non-flight spend.
- Card Type: Unsecured credit card.
- Annual Fee Waiver: Waived if total spend in the previous calendar year exceeds ₹100,000.
