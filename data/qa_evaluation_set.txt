--- NEW QUESTIONS (31–60) ---

-- Home Loan --
31. Q: What is the interest rate range for a NovaHome Loan (floating rate)? | A: 8.50 % – 10.00 % p.a. (linked to RBI Repo Rate)
32. Q: What is the maximum LTV ratio for a NovaHome Loan on a property worth ₹60 Lakh? | A: Up to 80 % (since property value is ≤ ₹75 Lakh)
33. Q: What is the processing fee for a NovaHome Loan? | A: 0.5 % of loan amount, minimum ₹5,000, maximum ₹25,000
34. Q: Is there a prepayment charge on a floating‑rate NovaHome Loan? | A: No, prepayment is free for floating‑rate home loans as per RBI guidelines
35. Q: What is the maximum tenure for a NovaHome Loan? | A: Up to 30 years (loan must be fully repaid by borrower's age 70)
36. Q: Under which Income Tax sections can NovaHome Loan borrowers claim tax benefits? | A: Principal repayment under Section 80C (up to ₹1.5 Lakh); interest under Section 24(b) (up to ₹2 Lakh for self‑occupied property)
37. Q: What is the minimum CIBIL score required for a NovaHome Loan? | A: 700 (680–699 eligible at a higher interest margin)

40. Q: What is the penalty for non‑maintenance of AMB in a NovaCurrent Account? | A: ₹1,000 per month for Regular; ₹2,500 per month for NovaCurrentPrime

42. Q: What is the daily online transaction limit for a NovaCurrentPrime account? | A: ₹25 Lakh per day
