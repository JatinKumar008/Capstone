## 3. NovaFixed Deposit (FD) – Various Tenures  

| Tenure | Interest Rate (p.a.)* | Minimum Deposit | Premature Withdrawal Penalty |
|--------|----------------------|----------------|------------------------------|
| 7 days – 6 months | 3.25 % – 3.75 % (sliding) | ₹10,000 | **0.5 %** of interest earned + 1 % of principal |
| 6 months – 1 year | 4.00 % – 4.50 % | ₹10,000 | **0.5 %** of interest earned + 1 % of principal |
| 1 year – 2 years | 5.00 % – 5.50 % | ₹10,000 | **1 %** of interest earned + 1 % of principal |
| 2 years – 3 years | 5.75 % – 6.25 % | ₹10,000 | **1 %** of interest earned + 1 % of principal |
| 3 years – 5 years | 6.50 % – 7.00 % | ₹10,000 | **1.5 %** of interest earned + 1 % of principal |
| 5 years – 10 years | 7.00 % – 7.25 % | ₹10,000 | **2 %** of interest earned + 1 % of principal |

\*Rates are **fixed** for the entire tenure; higher rates for senior citizens (+0.25 %).  

**General FD Terms**

- **Interest Payout Options** – *Quarterly*, *Half‑Yearly*, *Annual*, or *Maturity‑only* (default).  
- **Lock‑in** – Funds are locked for the chosen tenure; premature withdrawal allowed only after the first 7 days with penalty as above.  
- **Auto‑Renewal** – On maturity, the deposit can be auto‑renewed for the same tenure at prevailing rates unless the customer opts for payout.  
- **Nomination** – Must be provided at opening; can be updated anytime via net‑banking.  
- **Taxation** – TDS @ 10 % (or 20 % for NRI) on interest if interest exceeds **₹40,000** per FY.  
- **Documentation** – KYC (Aadhaar/PAN), address proof, and a **PAN** copy for interest credit.
