## 4. NovaFixed Deposit (FD) – Various Tenures  

| Tenure | Interest Rate (p.a.)* | Minimum Deposit | Premature Withdrawal Penalty |
|--------|----------------------|-----------------|------------------------------|
| 7 days – 6 months | 3.25 % – 3.75 % (sliding) | ₹10,000 | **0.5 %** of principal + loss of accrued interest |
| 6 months – 1 year | 4.00 % – 4.50 % | ₹10,000 | **0.75 %** of principal |
| 1 year – 2 years | 5.00 % – 5.50 % | ₹10,000 | **1.00 %** of principal |
| 2 years – 5 years | 5.75 % – 6.25 % | ₹10,000 | **1.25 %** of principal |
| 5 years – 10 years | 6.50 % – 7.00 % | ₹10,000 | **1.50 %** of principal |

\*Rates are **fixed** for the chosen tenure; higher rates are offered for senior citizens (+0.25 %).  

**General Terms & Conditions**

* **Interest Credit** – Paid **quarterly** (or annually on request).  
* **Auto‑Renewal** – Optional; if opted, the FD rolls over at the prevailing rate for the same tenure.  
* **Nomination** – Allowed at the time of opening or later via net‑banking.  
* **Partial Withdrawal** – Not permitted; only full closure allowed before maturity (subject to penalty).  
* **Loan Against FD** – Up to **80 %** of the FD value at 9 % p.a. (interest payable only on the loan amount).  
* **Documentation** – Valid KYC, PAN, and address proof.  
* **Tax** – TDS at 10 % (or 5 % for senior citizens) on interest ≥ ₹10,000 per FY, unless Form 15G/15H is submitted.
* **5‑Year Tax‑Saver FD** – Eligible for deduction under **Section 80C** (up to ₹1.5 Lakh); 5‑year lock‑in, no premature withdrawal allowed.

### General Disclosures for All NovaBank Products  

1. **Regulatory Compliance** – All products comply with RBI guidelines, the Banking Regulation Act, FEMA, and applicable Indian tax laws.  
2. **KYC Requirement** – Valid PAN, Aadhaar (or other approved OVD), and address proof required for all accounts. Re‑KYC every 2 years for NRI and high‑risk customers; every 10 years for low‑risk customers.  
3. **Grievance Redressal** – Customer complaints escalated to the Nodal Officer within 30 days. Unresolved complaints may be taken to the RBI Banking Ombudsman.  
4. **Interest Rate Changes** – Floating rates linked to the RBI Repo Rate and reset as per RBI guidelines. Fixed rates locked for the chosen tenure.  
5. **Account Dormancy** – Savings/Current accounts with no transactions for 12 months become **Inactive**; after 24 months, **Dormant**. Dormant accounts require branch re‑activation with KYC.  
6. **Nomination** – Strongly recommended for all deposit accounts. Nomination can be added/modified via net‑banking or branch.  
7. **DICGC Cover** – Deposits (Savings, FD, RD, Current) insured up to **₹5 Lakh** per depositor per bank under the Deposit Insurance and Credit Guarantee Corporation (DICGC).
