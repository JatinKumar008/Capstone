## 3. NovaFixed Deposit (FD) – Various Tenures  

| Tenure | Interest Rate (p.a.)* | Minimum Deposit | Premature Withdrawal Penalty |
|--------|----------------------|-----------------|------------------------------|
| 7 days – 6 months | 3.25 % – 3.75 % (sliding) | ₹10,000 | **0.5 %** of principal + loss of accrued interest |
| 6 months – 1 year | 4.00 % – 4.50 % | ₹10,000 | **0.75 %** of principal |
| 1 year – 2 years | 5.00 % – 5.50 % | ₹10,000 | **1.00 %** of principal |
| 2 years – 5 years | 5.75 % – 6.25 % | ₹10,000 | **1.25 %** of principal |
| 5 years – 10 years | 6.50 % – 7.00 % | ₹10,000 | **1.50 %** of principal |

\*Rates are **fixed** for the chosen tenure; higher rates are offered for senior citizens (+0.25 %).  

**General Terms & Conditions**

* **Interest Credit** – Paid **quarterly** (or annually on request).  
* **Auto‑Renewal** – Optional; if opted, the FD rolls over at the prevailing rate for the same tenure.  
* **Nomination** – Allowed at the time of opening or later via net‑banking.  
* **Partial Withdrawal** – Not permitted; only full closure allowed before maturity (subject to penalty).  
* **Loan Against FD** – Up to **80 %** of the FD value at 9 % p.a. (interest payable only on the loan amount).  
* **Documentation** – Valid KYC, PAN, and address proof.  
* **Tax** – TDS at 10 % (or 5 % for senior citizens) on interest ≥ ₹10,000 per FY, unless Form 15G/15H is submitted.

## 4. NovaRecurring Deposit (RD)  

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **Tenure Options** | 6 months, 12 months, 24 months, 36 months, 48 months, 60 months |
| **Monthly Installment** | Minimum **₹1,000** – Maximum **₹5 Lakh** (subject to KYC). |
| **Interest Rate (p.a.)** | 5.00 % – 6.00 % (sliding based on tenure). Senior citizens get +0.25 %. |
| **Maturity Benefit** | Principal + accrued interest paid on maturity (or transferred to a savings account). |
| **Premature Closure** | Allowed after **6 months** with a penalty of **1 %** of the total deposited amount plus loss of accrued interest. |
| **Lock‑in** | No lock‑in beyond the monthly installment schedule; missed instalments result in **₹100** penalty per missed month and the RD may be closed. |
| **Key Features** | • Automatic auto‑debit from linked NovaSavings/NovaPrime account. <br>• Option to convert to Fixed Deposit on maturity at prevailing FD rates. <br>• Free statement each quarter. |
| **Important T&C** | • All instalments must be received on the chosen date; otherwise, the RD is treated as a **regular deposit** with lower interest (3.75 %). <br>• Account may be frozen for KYC non‑compliance. <br>• Tax deducted at source (TDS) as per FD rules. |
