Below is a **comprehensive eligibility matrix** for the fictional Indian retail bank **NovaBank**.  
All figures are expressed in Indian Rupees (INR) and follow the typical regulatory framework used by Indian banks (RBI, KYC norms, etc.).  

---

## 1. NovaSavings Account  

| **Eligibility Parameter** | **Requirement** |
|---------------------------|-----------------|
| **Age** | 18 – 70 years (Minors 7‑17 can open a **Minor Savings Account** with a guardian’s consent) |
| **Resident Status** | Indian Resident (as per RBI definition). NRI can open a **NovaSavings NRI Account** (see NRI column). |
| **KYC Documents** | • **Resident:** PAN Card, Aadhaar (or Voter ID/Passport), Proof of Address (Utility bill, bank statement, or passport) <br>• **NRI:** Passport, OCI/PIO card (if applicable), PAN Card, overseas address proof, and a **NRE/NRO** bank statement. |
| **Initial Deposit** | Minimum **₹5,000** for a regular resident account. <br>**₹10,000** for a joint account. <br>**₹15,000** for an NRI account (NRE/NRO). |
| **Other Conditions** | No prior black‑listing by RBI or any other bank. Must not be a defaulter under any loan/overdraft facility with NovaBank. |

**Documents Required**  

| Document | Resident | NRI |
|----------|----------|-----|
| PAN Card | ✔ | ✔ |
| Aadhaar / Passport | ✔ (Aadhaar) | ✔ (Passport) |
| Proof of Address | ✔ (Utility bill, etc.) | ✔ (Overseas address proof + Indian address proof) |
| Photograph (2×2 cm) | ✔ | ✔ |
| Income proof (optional) | ✖ (not required) | ✔ (Salary slip/ITR for NRI) |
| Guardian’s ID (for minors) | ✔ | ✔ (if minor NRI) |

## 2. NovaPrime Savings Account  

A premium‑tier savings product targeted at salaried professionals and high‑net‑worth individuals.  

| **Eligibility Parameter** | **Requirement** |
|---------------------------|-----------------|
| **Minimum Monthly Average Balance (MAB)** | **₹50,000** (maintained across the month) |
| **Age** | 21 – 65 years |
| **Salary / Income Proof** | • **Salaried:** Minimum **₹6 Lakhs** annual taxable income (salary slips for last 3 months + Form 16). <br>• **Self‑Employed / Professional:** Minimum **₹8 Lakhs** annual net income (ITR for last 2 FYs + profit & loss statement). |
| **Resident Status** | Resident Indian only (NRI not eligible for NovaPrime). |
| **KYC** | Same as NovaSavings (PAN + Aadhaar + POA). |
| **Other** | Must not have any active defaulted loan/overdraft with NovaBank. |

**Documents Required**  

| Document | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| PAN Card | Mandatory |
| Aadhaar Card | Mandatory |
| Recent Passport‑size Photo | 2×2 cm |
| Salary Slips (last 3 months) **or** ITR (last 2 FYs) | For income verification |
| Form 16 (if salaried) | Shows TDS & taxable income |
| Bank Statements (last 3 months) | To verify MAB |
| Employment Letter (if required) | For self‑employed, a letter from a chartered accountant confirming income |

## 5. NovaPersonal Loan  

| **Eligibility Parameter** | **Requirement** |
|---------------------------|-----------------|
| **Age** | 21 – 60 years at loan disbursement. |
| **Employment Type** | • **Salaried (Private/Public)** <br>• **Self‑Employed / Professional** |
| **Minimum Monthly Income** | • **Salaried:** ≥ **₹25,000** net monthly (≈ ₹3 Lakhs p.a.). <br>• **Self‑Employed:** ≥ **₹40,000** net monthly (≈ ₹5 Lakhs p.a.) with at least 2 years of continuous operation. |
| **Credit Score (CIBIL)** | Minimum **750** for standard pricing. **720–749** eligible with higher interest margin. |
| **Employment Stability** | • Salaried: Minimum **12 months** continuous service with current employer (or 24 months if transferred within same group). <br>• Self‑Employed: Minimum **24 months** of GST returns/ITR filing. |
| **FOIR (Fixed‑Obligation‑to‑Income Ratio)** | ≤ **45 %** (total existing EMIs + proposed loan EMI). |
| **Maximum Loan Amount** | Up to **₹25 Lakhs** for salaried, **₹40 Lakhs** for self‑employed (subject to income & security). |
| **Residency** | Resident Indian. NRI personal loans are offered under a separate product (NovaNRI‑Loan). |
| **Other** | No pending defaults with NovaBank or any other bank; no criminal record; property or guarantor may be required for high‑value loans (>₹20 Lakhs). |

**Documents Required**  

| Document | Salaried | Self‑Employed |
|----------|----------|---------------|
| PAN Card | ✔ | ✔ |
| Aadhaar Card | ✔ | ✔ |
| Proof of Address | ✔ | ✔ |
| Salary Slips (last 3 months) | ✔ | ✖ |
| Form 16 (last FY) | ✔ | ✖ |
| Employment Letter / Relieving Letter (if applicable) | ✔ | ✖ |
| Bank Statements (last 6 months) | ✔ | ✔ |
| Income Tax Returns (ITR) – last 2 FYs | ✖ | ✔ |
| GST Returns (last 2 quarters) | ✖ | ✔ |
| Business Proof (Trade license, Udyog Aadhar) | ✖ | ✔ |
| Property Documents (if security required) | Optional | Optional |
| Passport‑size Photos (2×2 cm) | ✔ | ✔ |
| CIBIL Report (latest) | ✔ (bank obtains) | ✔ |

## 7. NovaCreditCard Platinum  

A premium credit‑card for high‑net‑worth individuals and long‑standing NovaBank customers.  

| **Eligibility Parameter** | **Requirement** |
|---------------------------|-----------------|
| **Age** | 21 – 60 years |
| **Minimum Annual Income** | **₹12 Lakhs** (≈ ₹1 Lakh monthly) for salaried; **₹18 Lakhs** for self‑employed (based on ITR). |
| **Credit Score (CIBIL)** | Minimum **800** (or 780 + with a **NovaBank Relationship Score** ≥ 85). |
| **Existing Relationship** | Must have at least **₹2 Lakhs** average annual balance across any NovaBank accounts (Savings, Current, Fixed Deposit) or have a **NovaPersonal Loan**/**NovaHome Loan** active for ≥ 2 years. |
| **Residency** | Resident Indian (NRI Platinum is a separate product). |
| **Other** | No defaults in the last 12 months; total outstanding credit card limit across all banks ≤ **₹5 Lakhs**. |
| **Annual Fee** | ₹2,500 (waived on spending ≥ ₹2 Lakhs per annum). |

**Documents Required**  

| Document | Description |
|----------|-------------|

| Aadhaar Card | Mandatory |
| Proof of Address | Mandatory |

| Property/Asset Proof (optional) | May be requested for very high‑limit cards |
| Passport‑size Photo (2×2 cm) | Mandatory |
