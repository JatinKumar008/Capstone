### Notes & Assumptions  

* All fees are **subject to change with a 30‑day notice** as per RBI guidelines.  
* GST (currently 18 %) is **included** in the amounts shown unless stated otherwise.  
* Senior citizens (≥ 60 years) enjoy additional waivers on ATM withdrawals and certain fees.  
* "Premium" customers (NovaPrime Savings, NovaCreditCard Platinum) receive the listed waivers automatically upon meeting the product‑specific eligibility criteria.  
* Mobile/internet banking transactions attract **zero fees** for most standard services; branch transactions may carry a nominal charge.  
* The fee schedule reflects typical Indian‑bank pricing in **FY 2024‑25** and is intended for illustrative purposes only.
